## How to read this

- The seed set is tiny (5 samples per category) and was written by the author of the rules. Treat the numbers as a smoke test of the harness, not as a measurement of real-world accuracy.
- For `description` and `result` samples only the detector can block, so `none` scores 0 there by construction.
- For `exfil_chain` samples the policy can block without any detector, but only if the content came from a tool listed under `untrusted_sources` in `eval/policy.yaml`.
